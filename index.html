FAQ

Common questions.

Which countries do you cover?

We work across Europe, with a strong base in Spain and Portugal, and support selected projects in South America. Tell us the locations in your brief and we confirm feasibility within 24h.

Can you handle surveys and permits before the install?

Yes. We can survey each site (measurements, photos, access, power) and manage landlord, mall, venue or municipal permits, plus H&S paperwork, so install day goes to plan.

How much notice do you need?

48h for standard retail installs once permits are in place. Stand builds and multi-country rollouts usually need at least 5 working days, depending on scope.

Do you work white-label under NDA?

Yes. We operate as your local team, under your brand. We sign an NDA on request and never publish or reference client projects.

What do we receive after the job?

Before/after photos by area or store, an issues log with resolutions, and a sign-off summary — delivered within 24–48h of close-out.

How do you quote?

Send us the locations, scope, dates and asset list. We reply within 24h with feasibility and a budget. No commitment needed.
